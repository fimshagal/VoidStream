once constructed, VoidStream refreshes its own pool in the background forever. there is no stop, no isRunning, no pull, no mix() — by design. ingress only happens through sources passed at construction.

coverage = share of available entropy sources that have ever delivered bytes into the pool. starts at 0% right after construction (pool is on bootstrap entropy only); first network fetch lands within ~1.5s, the rest stream in over time.
